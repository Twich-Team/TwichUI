# Healer Watch communication

Hover the live Healer Watch panel to open four optional **self-status** choices. Hovering sends nothing. Clicking Drinking, Out of mana, Need a moment or Ready attempts one short message about the player, never the watched healer. A three-second local interval limits repeated selections; there is no queue, retry, whisper or delivery confirmation.

The panel remains click-through: a motion-only child uses `SetMouseClickEnabled(false)` and `SetMouseMotionEnabled(true)` configured out of combat. The existing choose-healer and move interactions remain separate. The menu closes on panel hide, preview/move, group or profile changes, feature disable and combat. Solo, unavailable group/chat APIs, combat, chat lockdown and secret or invalid group data are refused locally; the current channel is rechecked on click. An instance group takes precedence even when it is a raid; otherwise raid or home-party chat is selected.

Source evidence: Gethe/wow-ui-source `forever` at [734f065329cff4992835b01a84f210a297db91b6](https://github.com/Gethe/wow-ui-source/tree/734f065329cff4992835b01a84f210a297db91b6): `SimpleScriptRegionAPIDocumentation.lua` documents the separate protected mouse setters; `Blizzard_CooldownViewer/CooldownViewer.lua` uses click-disabled motion for tooltips; `Blizzard_Gamepad/UI/Radials/GamepadRadial.lua` chooses a group channel for a selected radial message. `ChatInfoDocumentation.lua` marks `C_ChatInfo.SendChatMessage` restricted and documents no delivery return; `ChatConstantsDocumentation.lua` describes macro-message restrictions. These native uses do not prove addon handler permission or actual delivery.

**Verification:** offline tests exercise guarded routes, throttling, stale selection and hover without sending to the game. The simulator and real client were not available. Owner-directed native checks: verify click-through and hover/menu transition in normal, chooser and move states; deliberate click sends at most once on intended party/raid/instance channel; solo, combat and lockdown do not send; monitoring, alerts and profile/group lifecycle remain unchanged.
