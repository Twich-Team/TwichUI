# TwichUI: What is it?
This addon was developed to consolidate several common tweaks, and provide several small, thoughtful touches that should help players read the game and make their own decisions while preserving the atmosphere, discovery and sense of place that make Forever compelling. Offering elegant, unobtrusive features familiar to RPGs, such as zone arrival messages, friend notifications, available training, and journey chronicling.

In addition, several features exist to dive deep into your addon settings and configurations, create backup snapshots, export and import, as well as share your configuration with your friends through in-game addon channels.

The addon is intended to work alongside EllesmereUI.

## Features


Every feature has its own page in the TwichUI settings (`/tui options`), and every one can be turned off. Anything that acts for you is opt-in.

### Moments that mark the journey
- **Journey Chronicle:** A private, per-character journal that records levels, new zones, boss kills, professions and more as you play. Add your own notes, filter by date, and restyle the window to taste. It is never shared or sent anywhere.
- **Arrival cards:** A brief title card when you reach a new zone, and an optional Welcome Back bookmark when you return to a character.
- **Training notices:** A quiet note at level-up when you have new abilities to learn.
- **Friend logins:** Battle.net friends logging in appear as a small card instead of a chat line.
- **Notification look:** Choose fonts, colors, opacity and backdrop for the cards, shared or per card.

### Map pins
- **Local and party pins:** Drop named pins on your world map. Local pins are yours alone; party pins are shared with party members who also run TwichUI, for as long as you're grouped.
- **Keep what matters:** Mark a pin as kept to protect it, share a saved pin with your group, or make pins from `/way` commands.
- **Where you want them:** Pins show on the minimap and a data bar launcher, with an optional sound when one arrives.

### Gear
- **Upgrade hints:** Quiet "likely upgrade" lines in item tooltips and small markers in your bags, based on your class and talent tree. Hold Shift to see why. It's a rough guide, not a simulation.
- **Stat weights:** Adjust how each talent tree values stats if you follow a guide.

### Everyday conveniences
- **Food and Drink buttons:** Two buttons that eat or drink the best food or drink in your bags when you click them. They sit nicely with EllesmereUI's Unlock Mode.
- **Quality of life, all opt-in:**
  - Accept summons and resurrections from people you choose.
  - Release your spirit in battlegrounds.
  - Decline duel requests.
  - Easy Fishing: one key to cast and reel in.
  - A Quick Keybind button in the Game Menu.
  - Show the game's waypoint on the place RestedXP's arrow is leading you.
- **Auction House:** A Sell from Bags tab that suggests a price from current listings. You set quantity, price and duration; nothing posts until you press Post.
- **Quiet login and fonts:** Hide addon "loaded" messages, and add Alegreya, Barlow, Cinzel and Spectral fonts plus bell sounds to your font and sound lists.

### Mages
- **Travel and Conjuring launchers:** Data bar launchers for your teleports and portals and for your best Conjure Food and Conjure Water.
- **Refreshments panel:** Works out how much food and water your party or raid needs, lets you conjure by click or key, and keeps a checklist of who you've supplied.
- **Trade help:** Fill a trade with the planned refreshments. Trades are never accepted for you.
- **Sell leftover food and drink:** Optionally sell ordinary, non-conjured food and drink to a vendor, with a dry-run mode that only reports what would be sold. With EllesmereUI's bags you can instead have TwichUI mark those items as junk there, and let EllesmereUI sell them.

### Addon skins
- **EllesmereUI look:** Matching skins for Attune, Auctionator, the Forever Dungeon Journal and WhatsTraining (needs EllesmereUI).
- **RestedXP guide window:** A TwichUI look for the window, its waypoint arrow and its timer bars.
- **SpeakStone:** A TwichUI look for SpeakStone's speech frame.

### Settings, profiles and sharing
- **One settings window:** Searchable, organized by feature, and only loaded when you open it.
- **Profiles:** Save sets of TwichUI options. Each character picks a profile; characters can share one or keep their own.
- **Configuration sharing:** Send your TwichUI settings, other addons' settings and an EllesmereUI profile to friends through in-game addon channels. Everything is previewed and confirmed before it's applied, with a way to undo.
- **Backups and restore points:** Snapshot your addon settings and restore them later.
- **Saved Data Explorer:** A read-only look at what TwichUI and other addons have saved, with optional, confirmed cleanup.
- **Troubleshooting:** A status snapshot (versions, whether TwichUI and its optional addons are set up and reachable), a short support report to read and copy when something isn't working, and a fuller diagnostic report. Nothing is sent anywhere.

### Works with
EllesmereUI, RestedXP Guides, SpeakStone, Attune, Auctionator, WhatsTraining and Forever Dungeon Journal. All are optional; TwichUI runs fine without any of them.
