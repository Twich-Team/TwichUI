## **Features**

<div><strong>Fonts and sounds.</strong> Adds various thematic fonts to the game, allowing them to be</div>

<div>utilized for interface customization.</div>

  
**Quiet login.** Attempts to hide all addon welcome messages when logging in to

<div>prevent being brought out of immersion by being inundated with unnecessary information.</div>

 

<div><strong>Upgrade hints</strong>. When something you could wear looks better than what you</div>

<div>have, its tooltip gets one quiet line such as "Likely upgrade for Fury". Hold</div>

<div>Shift (your compare-items key) to see why: what it's compared with, the stat</div>

<div>changes that mattered most and which weights were used. Items that aren't</div>

<div>upgrades, or that you can't use, get nothing unless you ask.&nbsp;</div>

undefined

<div><strong>Addon skins.</strong> Provides EllemereUI skinning to various addons to create a cohesive look and feel. Currently supprts: Attune, Auctionator, DungeonJournal, and WhatsTraining.</div>

 

<div><strong>Configuration sharing</strong>. Provides an ecosystem to share your addon configurations with friends.</div>
