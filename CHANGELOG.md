# TwichUI changelog

## Unreleased
- **Mage refreshments** (new, for Mages, off until you turn it on; **experimental: needs checking in the game**; developed on its own branch, not part of the 3.0.9 release candidate): a **Refreshments** panel (`/tui refreshments`, Key Bindings > TwichUI, the Mage Conjuring menu, or **Mage > Refreshments panel**) for feeding and watering your group. **Refreshment shares** (a page under Mage) sets how many single items of water and food each class gets in a party and in a raid, and how many you keep; 0 gives none, and the starting amounts are only a starting point. The panel plans each member's share at the best rank their level can use (your own best rank when their level can't be read), adds what you keep, and shows it against your bags with the casts still needed once one cast's yield has been seen, and whether your bags have room. Its **Water** and **Food** buttons are the game's own secure buttons: a click or their key casts the rank still needed **once per press**, on the release, so dragging one never casts it; drag one to an action bar to hold a key with the game's Press and Hold Casting, which the game only does from its own bars. TwichUI can't stop a held cast at a target: the bar turns green and one line says "Enough water prepared." so you can let go; the last cast may give a few extra. The **Group** list shows each member's share and what you've marked as handed over (**Supplied**, **Clear**, a stack more or less), and marks the next person not yet supplied. Kept by GUID in memory only: party to raid, leaving and rejoining keep it; leaving the group, a reload or **Reset session** start it again; names are never saved. The panel can't be opened in combat and closes as combat starts; the buttons' spell changes only out of combat. Nothing listens on other classes or while it is off. A **Mage refreshments** section in `/tui diagnostics` gives counts and item IDs, never names. **Trade assistance** (**Fill trade button and trade tracking**, on with the panel): trading with a group member, whoever opened the trade, shows a strip under the trade window with what goes in for them and a **Fill trade** button. **Fill trades automatically** (on): their share goes in by itself a moment after the trade opens; it stops for that trade if you take something out, never adds once either of you has pressed Trade, waits while you hold something or are in combat, and if the game doesn't allow it, switches itself off until you reload (one line says so) and the button still works. **Each trade puts in**: their whole share every trade (the default, for when they come back for more; deliveries add up in the checklist), or only what is still owed this session. Each fill puts in whole stacks of the rank planned for them into empty slots (never the seventh, never over or out of anything already there), and a part stack split into an empty bag slot, added once the game shows it. What is already in the window counts towards the share; it never puts in anything else, never acts with something on the cursor or in combat, and never accepts. A trade is counted as handed over only when the game says it was completed, from your side of the window as you accepted it; when TwichUI can't confirm it, the checklist shows **Offered?** with **Confirm** and **Dismiss**. People outside your group get no share. Diagnostics show which trade-complete message the client has and the codes seen, to check in the game. **Data bar plugin**: "TwichUI Mage Refreshments" for EllesmereUI's Broker Plugin block or any LibDataBroker data bar, made once refreshments are on (no reload): its text is how many of your group are supplied ("3/5 supplied"), "Refreshments", or nothing (**Text on the data bar** on the Mage page); its tooltip sums up water and food prepared, who is supplied and who is next; click opens the panel, right-click the Mage options. **Refreshments panel appearance** (Mage page): the panel and its trade strip take your background texture, color and opacity, and border texture, thickness, color and opacity, with a preview (the real panel beside the options) and a reset; the same textures as the broker menus, saved separately. **Background textures** (panel and broker menus) now include the textures EllesmereUI offers for its own backgrounds (Melli, Plating, Glass, Matte, Pixels Background and the rest, while EllesmereUI is installed) and LibSharedMedia's status bar textures, as EllesmereUI lists them; before, only the game's own and LibSharedMedia's background textures were offered. They are stretched and tinted by the background color, as EllesmereUI draws them. Borders: the game's own, EllesmereUI's own border textures and LibSharedMedia's. A thick border never covers the panel's content. The broker menus' appearance is unchanged; its settings code is now shared with the panel's.
- **Release hardening** (**needs checking in the game**): TwichUI's windows (sharing and backups, addon list, backup export/import, saved data, Chronicle and its note editor, Troubleshooting, Addon data, Welcome) now shrink just enough to fit a small game window or high UI scale, and return to full size when there is room; text is never resized on its own and cards and movers keep their saved places. A backup name or addon title that is cut short in a list shows in full when you hover it. With upgrade marks on bags switched off (the default), a bag redraw no longer looks up every slot's item, and a group roster change no longer starts a timer while the group check is off. Nothing you saved changes. Backups you create yourself still have no limit on how many (imports are capped at 20); this is flagged in `docs/release-hardening.md` for a decision, along with the supported-client scope, profiling steps and the manual checklist. New test: `tests/test_release.lua`.
- **Welcome dialog and clearer first run** (new, **needs checking in the game**): a new installation opens one short, fully styled **Welcome to TwichUI** page, once for the whole account, a few seconds after the world loads. It says what TwichUI is, points to the settings, the notification previews (Notifications page) and the troubleshooting report, has **Open Settings** and **Close** buttons and the usual close box and Escape, and reminds you of `/tui`. It changes no setting. It is drawn with the Chronicle's umber and bronze and the zone card's fonts, so it looks the same with or without EllesmereUI. It waits for combat, flight paths, cinematics, a hidden interface, loading screens and any TwichUI card (Welcome Back, zone, training, friend) to be out of the way, never holds back or discards a card, looks a bounded number of times, and is marked as shown only once it is really on screen. **Only a load that finds no saved settings at all is a new installation** (`TwichUIDB.welcome.state`, written by `Persist.lua`); upgrades, damaged or newer saves are never put through it, and switching characters, profiles or resetting a feature does not bring it back. **`/tui about`** (or **Help > Welcome > Show** in the options overview) shows it again; `/tui welcome` is still the Chronicle's Welcome Back preview. The options overview also has **Chat commands > List**. No default changed. Gear comparison's text now says plainly that nothing has to be entered (TwichUI starts from its own rough weights), how the talent tree is chosen automatically and where to override it, and that a class without weights gets no hints; the README no longer says the Chronicle is off by default and never announced in chat (it is on by default, with an optional line in your own chat). New test: `tests/test_welcome.lua`; `docs/lifecycle.md` has the manual checklist.
- **Saved data** (hardening; **needs checking in the game**): TwichUI's saved settings now carry a schema number (`TwichUIDB.schema`, separate from the release version), and upgrades, defaults and damaged data are handled in one place (`Persist.lua`; `docs/persistence.md` lists every saved variable, its owner, the upgrade path, retention and the in-game test). No setting, saved place, backup, setup or Chronicle entry changes for anyone: the releases 3.0.4 to 3.0.8 were checked by loading what each one actually wrote. What is new: **(1)** the clean-ups that ran on every load (sending method from the old group and guild switches, the removed combo points keys, leftovers of the addon-data scan) are now one numbered, repeatable upgrade step that runs on a working copy and is only kept if the result is usable; if it fails nothing is changed and it is tried again next load. **(2)** Defaults fill only what is missing; `false`, `0` and empty values stay. An on/off setting holding something else is put back to its default instead of switching the feature on; a settings group that isn't a table is rebuilt without touching its neighbours; setups, backups and restore points that can't be read are repaired or dropped one at a time, never the whole set. Values that a feature judges itself (sizes, textures, positions) are still not erased when they are out of range. **(3)** Saved data from a **newer** TwichUI is never changed or downgraded: it is set aside for the session and given back exactly as it was at logout, with one notice (settings and Chronicle each). **(4)** Setups from friends, pasted backups, restore points and files now go through the same checks (a valid table name, the right shape, plain data of bounded depth and size) when they arrive and again when they are applied; a setup that named a game table such as `_G`, `string` or `SlashCmdList` is now refused, where it used to pass. Restore points, your shareable setup and the last search no longer share tables, importing a backup can't be clicked twice, and its limits are checked again at the click. Backups made by earlier versions import the same way (the export format is unchanged). **(5)** Chronicle: a note is never dropped for a repeated or missing id, a missing title or a missing date (it keeps no date rather than an invented one); entries of a kind this version doesn't know (written by a newer one) are kept aside unchanged; unknown fields are kept. Text over the limits is still shortened on load, as before. **(6)** `/tui diagnostics` has a **Saved data** section: stored and current versions, what happened, and a count per repair by code, with no contents. One chat notice at login, and only when a stored value had to be reset or set aside, a newer save was left alone, or an upgrade failed. Nothing is deleted for age or size; restore points and Chronicle characters still only grow when you add to them (see the retention notes in the document for what is proposed). Not covered: whether the game manages to write the files (a crash or a full disk), and the one assumption the tests can't check: that the game saves after `PLAYER_LOGOUT`.
- **Interaction details** (**needs checking in the game**): a pass over how existing controls behave, with no new features and no change to how anything looks apart from the states below. Confirmations (apply, undo, restore, delete, Chronicle delete, stat-weight reset, clear and refill) now say what changes, which setup, backup, tree or entry is affected and whether it can be undone, and accepting is checked against what is there at that moment: if a friend's newer setup arrives, or the setup, backup or entry is deleted, while the question is open, nothing is applied and you are told why. A question can be accepted only once, cancelling changes nothing, and actions that reload the UI refuse in combat. Resetting or clearing a talent tree's stat weights now acts on the tree the question named, even if another is chosen while it is open (before, it used whichever was showing when you answered); a stat-weight box you are still typing in is kept for its own tree first, and a rejected number names the stat and what you typed. Creating a backup that needs a reload now asks first, like scanning does. In the Chronicle, an empty note is no longer closed and thrown away silently (Save is off and Enter says why), the delete question quotes the entry, buttons have pressed and disabled looks, and a second Enter can't save a note twice. Tooltips: buttons explain why they are unavailable even while dimmed (Send, Test on myself, Check this party, Import as backup, Reset weights, Add a stat, Clear list, Clear records), rows greyed out in the options say what they need, options that take effect after a reload say so, and tooltips go away with the control that owns them (hiding a window or button, Edit Mode closing, the calendar closing, combat starting or ending under the pointer on the Quick Keybind button, a Food or Drink choice changing or waiting for combat). The Food and Drink tooltip now says what a click does and when an update is waiting for combat to end. Notification previews wait for combat and say when one could not be shown, the sharing page's Open button is greyed out while sharing is off, and Clear records in the options and the troubleshooting window answer when used. The shared pieces are in `modules/Interact.lua`.
- **Notifications: previews and taking turns** (**needs checking in the game**): the **Notifications** options page has a new **Notification previews** section: a button each for the zone arrival, new training, friend login and Welcome Back cards, **Test coordinated sequence** and **Clear previews**, and a **Play notification sounds in previews** switch (off by default; the friend chime then plays only while its own chime option is on, as for a real login). A preview is the real card, in your chosen place and style, with made-up details; it tests how a card looks, not whether the game event is noticed. It works with that card switched off, shows above the options window, records and changes nothing (no Chronicle entry, no friend or training state, no saved place), and gives way to any real notice. The four cards now take turns through one small coordinator (`modules/Notify.lua`): cards in different parts of the screen no longer wait for each other (the friend card shows beside the zone card), while cards that would overlap (the zone, training and Welcome Back cards share the top by default; moving them in Edit Mode changes who waits for whom) show one at a time, first come first served. A notice that waits too long, a place you have already left, a repeat of the same event, and anything left over from a loading screen are dropped rather than shown late or stacked up; at most six wait. The Welcome Back card gives way to any other card that needs its place. Sounds play when a card appears, not when it is queued. `/tui friend`, `/tui welcome` and `/tui training` are now previews too (silent unless previews are allowed sound). There is no friend-logout card, so none is previewed.
- **Quality of life** (new, all off until you turn them on; **needs checking in the game**): a **Quality of life** options page with four independent conveniences, each doing only what it says and listening for its events only while on. **Accept summons** accepts an ordinary summon from friends, guild and group members (or group members only, or anyone) after a short wait during which the game's own pop-up stays up; scenario and starting-area summons are always left to you, nothing is accepted in combat or when the game won't let you teleport, and there is no retry. **Accept resurrection** accepts an offer you receive, keeps the game's own waiting time, leaves offers that carry resurrection sickness to you, and accepts a combat resurrection only with its separate option on (judged by an offerer in your group being in combat; the game doesn't flag them). **Release in battlegrounds** releases your spirit after a short wait, in battlegrounds only (never dungeons, raids, arenas, the open world or Hardcore), never with a self-resurrection or a resurrection on offer (an offer during the wait cancels it), and Shift as the wait ends keeps your body. **Decline duel requests** declines ordinary duels except from the people you allow (friends, guild and group members by default), with a separate option for duels to the death. The two waits are under Advanced. Overlap with Leatrix Plus is noted in each tooltip, never changed. **Not offered:** Leatrix Plus's "Block requested invites" (it declines the "X requests that you invite Y" prompt, `GROUP_INVITE_CONFIRMATION`), because the Forever UI source has neither that prompt nor `GetInviteConfirmationInfo` / `RespondToInviteConfirmation`; arenas and open-world PvP for releasing, because the game gives no reliable way to tell those contexts apart or to know releasing is wanted there.
- **Configuration sharing and EllesmereUI** (fix; **needs checking in the game**): sharing used to copy EllesmereUI's whole saved table, `EllesmereUIDB`, which holds every profile, which profile each spec uses, keybinds, the Cooldown Manager spell store, click-cast and per-character names, gold and specs (its other saved tables are empty leftovers). Applying a friend's setup replaced all of that, and sending one shared all of it. Now: the whole table is never sent or applied. **Share setup > EllesmereUI profile** picks one profile, which EllesmereUI itself exports (all installed modules, the profile's look (fonts, custom colours, dark mode, accent), layout links, Cooldown Manager spells and overrides; no UI scale, window skins or spec assignments). A friend's **Received setups > EllesmereUI profile...** window shows the source profile, the exact new name (never an existing one; "Raid (Rich)", then "Raid (Rich) 2"...), what is included and left out, and asks to confirm before EllesmereUI's own import adds it as a **new profile**. EllesmereUI has no import that leaves the active profile alone, so the new profile becomes the one in use and the review says so (their previous profile stays saved and unchanged; if the current spec has its own assigned profile, the new one is saved and not switched to). Nothing existing is ever overwritten or deleted, including a profile imported earlier: importing the same profile again makes another copy, and TwichUI records where each import came from (in its own data, not EllesmereUI's) to say so. TwichUI doesn't update an imported profile in place, because EllesmereUI has no way to replace one without deleting its spec assignments and keybind, and can't tell edits from its own changes. A failed import removes only what it made and puts the previous profile back. Older setups that carry the whole database are never applied as a table: the sender's active profile's module settings are pulled out and offered the same way, or the setup says why it can't be. One effect of the look: when EllesmereUI imports custom colours it points every "shared palette" colour section at the imported profile, so profiles that pull colours from a shared source show the new palette; the review says so. Backups still keep EllesmereUI's table and restore it whole, on purpose, and say so before restoring. Needs checking in the game: the import itself (EllesmereUI's functions were read from its source, not run), activation and reload, and the spec-assigned case.
- **Troubleshooting** (new, **needs checking in the game**): one place to look when something doesn't work, in place of the scattered temporary commands. **`/tui diagnostics`** (or **Troubleshooting > Open** on a new options page of that name) opens a small window with a report you can read and copy by hand (click in the text, Ctrl+A, Ctrl+C; the game gives an addon no clipboard, so there is no copy button). It says which version of TwichUI and of the game you have, which optional addons and libraries are there, how EllesmereUI is joined up, and for each part (notification coordination, Battle.net friend cards, the Food and Drink buttons, the zone arrival card, the Chronicle's zone entries, configuration sharing) whether it is switched on, whether it started, and what it says about itself: ready, waiting, unavailable or off, with a short reason code. "Ready" means the module believes it can work, not that it was seen working. The report is built when you open the window or press **Refresh report**, never in the background, and a part that is missing or breaks only loses its own section. **`/tui diagnostics start`** begins temporary tracing, **`stop`** ends it, **`clear`** forgets what was collected, **`status`** prints a short summary in chat. Tracing is off until you start it; it keeps at most 200 short records in memory (96 characters each, repeats counted, a rate limit on floods), stops by itself after 15 minutes, is never saved and is gone after a reload. While it runs it records TwichUI's own decisions (why a friend card was skipped, what the Food and Drink buttons chose and when they waited for combat, whether a zone change counted as an arrival or was ignored on a flight path, what the notification coordinator queued, showed and let go, how a configuration transfer progressed) and listens, only then, for the few game events those need (friend events; dying, resurrecting, combat and bags for the Food and Drink buttons). Tests and previews (`/tui diagnostics test friend`, `test sound [stock]`, a settings preview, `/tui share test`) are marked SYNTHETIC wherever they appear: they show a card or a send works, not that a real event or another player got through. The report holds codes, counts and anonymous labels (F1, P2...), never character, realm, guild, friend or BattleTag names, chat, Chronicle entries or notes, configuration contents or full friend records, and nothing is ever sent or posted. It also lists the last few errors that TwichUI's own event and startup handlers caught (the game's error handler still gets them unchanged); that is not every error, and it says so: for full capture use an error display addon such as BugSack. **Replaces `/tui frienddiag`** (still accepted; it points to the new commands): its event trace, synthetic card and chime tests and report are now part of this. `/tui probe` (the Food and Drink item check, also `/tui diagnostics probe`) and `/tui commtest` stay as they were; `/tui share status` and `/tui skin` are unchanged. Not covered: the report can't tell whether a card looked right, only what the code decided. The friend card doesn't keep a snapshot of who was online, so none is reported.
- **Addon data** (new, **experimental**: needs checking in the game): a read-only window, `/tui data` (or **Addon data > Open** on the Configuration sharing options page), that shows what your addons keep between sessions. On the left, each installed addon with where it stands for this character (Loaded, Loads when needed, Off for this character, Off, Out of date, Not installed) and the data it keeps; on the right, the chosen item's contents as a tree you open a level at a time (200 entries at a time, with **Show more**), its entry count, and an estimate of its size if saved as it is now (some addons trim their data as they save it, so the file can be smaller). Color codes, icons and links in saved text are shown as text, never used; a table that contains itself isn't opened, and one that appears in more than one place says so. The game doesn't say which data belongs to which addon, so **Find addon data** reloads your UI once and notes which plain data (not code) appears as each addon loads, the moment setup sharing already uses: a very likely match, not a certain one, and the window says so. Only names and owners are remembered, never the data; finds for addons that didn't load are kept and marked. What it can't do, by the game's design: show data of addons that are off, waiting to load or removed (the game doesn't load it), read or measure the files on disk, tell account-wide from per-character data, or change or delete another addon's data. Sizes are counted when the window opens, a little each frame. TwichUI's own data is listed from its own declaration, with a link to its existing Saved data list for deleting. Nothing is changed, copied or sent. A scan also picks up some of each addon's own working data (texture lists, caches, constants its code makes every session); items whose names suggest that (starting with _, in capitals, or named after the addon itself) are listed apart under **Probably working data**, folded, and left out of the addon's total. Checked against the saved files on the Forever beta, those signs caught 24 of 26 such items and no saved ones; two still show as saved data. For exact answers and for files left behind by removed addons, see `tools/sv_inspect.py` below.
- **Configuration sharing**: an addon turned off for this character only was shown as ready to receive settings, and a load-on-demand addon that hadn't loaded yet as turned off. The game's addon info answers for all characters and calls load-on-demand addons "not loadable" until they load; TwichUI now asks about this character and treats load-on-demand addons as ready (their settings apply when they load, as before).
- **`tools/sv_inspect.py`** (new, for your computer, not the game; needs Python 3): reads WoW's SavedVariables files with the game closed and lists, per account and character, each addon's saved variables, their exact sizes on disk, and which files belong to addons not installed in that game folder, with the evidence and what's uncertain. It reads the files as data and never runs them. It changes nothing unless you ask it to move a not-installed addon's files to a quarantine folder (a dry run first, then a typed confirmation, only with WoW closed), from where `restore` puts them back. Nothing is uploaded.
- **Food and Drink buttons**: new option **Prefer Mage-conjured food and water** (off by default, so nothing changes unless you turn it on). With it on, a Mage-conjured item you can use is chosen over ordinary food or drink even when the ordinary item restores more health or mana; food and water are chosen separately (conjured food but no conjured water gives the conjured food and the best ordinary drink), among conjured items the usual ranking applies, and with none of a kind the button chooses as before. It works for any character carrying conjured items, and it never lets in an item the buttons would skip (unusable, above your level, buff food, feasts). Conjured items are recognized by item ID (the six ranks of Conjured Food and Conjured Water; the game gives no "conjured" flag), not by name, so it holds in any language. The choice is made again when you change the option or your bags change, and as before nothing changes in combat. The button's tooltip says when it holds an item because of this option. Needs checking in the game: the item IDs are the Classic-era ones and have not been confirmed against the Forever client (`/tui probe` shows an item's ID).
- **Broker menu appearance** (Mages, on the **Mage** options page): the menus Mage Travel and Mage Conjuring open from your data bar now share a **Broker menu appearance** section: **Background texture**, **color** and **opacity**, and **Border texture**, **thickness**, **color** and **opacity**, with **None** for either texture. The defaults are the look the menus already had, and nothing is saved until you change something. Textures are the game's own (Tooltip, Dialog, Marble and Rock backgrounds; Tooltip and Dialog borders) and anything in LibSharedMedia's *background* and *border* lists, so another addon's media pack shows up here too, also when it loads after TwichUI. With EllesmereUI installed, its own border textures (such as Pixels Textured) are listed too, drawn by EllesmereUI the way the Food and Drink buttons do (it has no background textures to offer); LibSharedMedia's Blizzard textures from other versions of the game, which this client may not have, are not offered. Nothing needs EllesmereUI. Background and border opacity are separate, and neither fades the menu's text, icons or buttons. Border thickness is in screen pixels (0 to 16), so it looks the same at any UI scale, and the menu keeps its text clear of a thick border. A texture that is no longer available is drawn as a plain color or line and stays chosen. **Preview** shows a sample menu beside the options that follows your changes; **Reset** puts back only this appearance. An open menu follows a change at once, but nothing changes in combat: the menu is drawn afresh when you next open it. What the menus do, and how they cast, is unchanged. Needs checking in the game.
- **Mage Conjuring**: the menu now shows one entry each for **Food** and **Water**, the highest rank you have learned of each (they can differ), instead of every rank. The rank is found by asking the spellbook about each rank's own spell in order, and clicking casts that exact spell through the game's own secure button; if the highest rank's name hasn't loaded yet, its entry waits for it rather than offering a lower rank. With no rank of one learned, its first rank is shown muted with the level it is trained at. The menu is still read when it opens, redrawn while open when you learn a spell, and never changed in combat. The launcher's tooltip still counts "N of 6 learned". Needs checking in the game. Lower ranks can no longer be picked from the menu.
- **Configuration sharing** now sends **Direct** by default: straight to the character you name, with no group or guild needed. **Party** and **Guild** are still there, as an explicit choice: **Sending options** in the sharing window, or **Send configurations by** under Advanced on the Configuration sharing page. TwichUI uses only the one you pick; if it fails, you're told why and nothing is sent another way. **Receiving works on all three** whatever you send with (offers still ask first, and nothing is applied until you click Apply). Party and Guild messages reach everyone in that group; only the named friend's TwichUI reads them, but that is a filter, not privacy. The three old "allow direct / group / guild" switches are replaced by this one choice. **Upgrading:** new installs, and anyone who left the old defaults, get Direct. If you had switched on the group channel you stay on Party; if you had switched on the guild channel you stay on Guild (both: Party). The old settings can't tell a deliberate choice from a workaround for the beta's broken direct messages, so nothing is guessed; pick Direct in Sending options whenever you like. The message format is unchanged, but TwichUI versions before this one only read Party or Guild messages if their owner had switched those on. Sending also now notices when the game refuses a message, and a transfer that was fully sent but never confirmed by the friend now ends with a clear message instead of waiting forever. The test message sent once per game build only reports whether direct messages got through; it no longer changes how anything is sent. `/tui commtest` accepts `direct` as well as `whisper`, and says it checks one small round trip only.
- **Mage Travel** (new, for Mages, on by default; **experimental**: needs checking in the game): a LibDataBroker launcher, **TwichUI Mage Travel**, for your data bar addon, with its own icon (a swirling blue portal in a gold ring, `media/textures/mage-travel.tga`) (TwichUI doesn't bundle LibDataBroker; with no data bar addon there is nowhere for it to show). Clicking it opens a small menu in the Chronicle's colors with **Teleports** and **Portals** for your faction's three capitals, the ones WoW: Forever teaches. Click a learned one to cast it: each row is the game's own secure action button, the click is yours, and the menu closes after the cast. Ones you haven't learned are muted but readable and do nothing when clicked; their tooltip shows the game's spell tooltip, then the level they are trained at (from What's Training?'s Forever data, MIT, already bundled for New training) and "Learning location unavailable", since no source TwichUI has records where Forever teaches them. It can't be opened in combat (it says so where the game shows its own error messages), closes as combat starts, and closes on Esc or a click elsewhere. Its contents are read when it opens and updated while open when you learn a spell or level up. New **Mage** options page (Mages only) with **Mage Travel launcher on my data bar** and **Text on the data bar**: **Travel** (the default), **Portals** or **None (icon only)**, changed at once. Nothing is saved but those two, nothing is sent, and it has no effect on Broker_Portals.
- **Mage Conjuring** (new, for Mages, on by default; **experimental**: needs checking in the game): a second launcher, **TwichUI Mage Conjuring**, beside Mage Travel and built the same way (the two now share one menu, `modules/SpellMenu.lua`). Its menu has **Food** and **Water** sections with each rank of Conjure Food and Conjure Water WoW: Forever teaches (six of each), highest first, so you can also conjure a lower rank for a lower-level friend. Click a learned rank to cast it through the game's own secure button. Ranks you haven't learned are muted but readable; their tooltip shows the game's spell tooltip, the level they are trained at, the rank you need first if you don't have it yet, and "Taught by Mage trainers". Its data bar icon is its own (a loaf and a corked blue flask, `media/textures/mage-conjure.tga`), and **Text on the data bar** offers **Conjure** (the default), **Food & Water** or **None (icon only)**. It follows the same rules as Mage Travel: it can't be opened in combat, closes as combat starts, on Esc, on a click elsewhere or after a cast, and listens for spell changes only while open. On the **Mage** options page (renamed from Mage travel): **Mage Conjuring launcher on my data bar** and its text choice. Ranks and levels: What's Training?'s Forever data (MIT), already bundled.
